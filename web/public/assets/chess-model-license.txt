The game's six chess piece meshes are adapted from this CC0 asset.
The game supplies its own materials and procedural board; Poly Haven's
texture files and website preview renders are not distributed in the game.
Adaptations: piece extraction, coordinate/scale normalization and newly
generated collision hulls, Knight orientation and physical mass calibration.
Source hashes and the reproducible conversion
command are recorded in assets-source/polyhaven-chess in the project.
