Chess Alkkagi Privacy Policy

This page covers Chess Alkkagi played on CrazyGames. CrazyGames handles its own platform data under its separate privacy policy.

Information used by the game

If you are signed in to CrazyGames, the game uses your CrazyGames account identifier to connect to a server-bound Chess Alkkagi account. The game does not receive your CrazyGames email address or password. Supabase provides authentication and stores the linked account ID, player name, and account progress such as unlocked stages and permanent research. Session tokens are used to keep you signed in.

This candidate offers solo play. It does not open multiplayer matchmaking or peer connections. Earlier account and match records are not deleted by this update.

Your browser also stores game progress and settings locally. In-progress runs and their selected cards resume from the start of a stage in the same browser, separately for each linked account or guest. These run checkpoints are not synced across devices. Clearing site data removes local records but does not delete the server account or its saved data. This CrazyGames build does not include in-game ads.

Access and deletion

To request access, correction, or deletion of your Chess Alkkagi account or match data on our servers, email dubokup@gmail.com. We verify the requester and explain the scope and result of the request. Do not send passwords or session tokens.
