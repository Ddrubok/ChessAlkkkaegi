Chess Alkkagi Privacy Policy

This page covers Chess Alkkagi played on CrazyGames. CrazyGames handles its own platform data under its separate privacy policy.

Information used by the game

When you play online, the game uses your CrazyGames account identifier to sign you into a server-bound Chess Alkkagi account. The game does not receive your CrazyGames email address or password. Supabase provides authentication and stores the linked account ID, player name, rankings, match results, and account progress such as stages and upgrades. Session tokens are used to keep you signed in.

Online matches use WebRTC. Connection setup can expose network addresses and connection metadata to the other player and connection services. A TURN relay may be used when a direct connection is unavailable.

Your browser also stores game progress and settings locally. Clearing site data removes those local records but does not delete the server account or its saved data. This CrazyGames build does not include in-game ads.

Access and deletion

To request access, correction, or deletion of your Chess Alkkagi account or match data on our servers, email dubokup@gmail.com. We verify the requester and explain the scope and result of the request. Do not send passwords or session tokens.
