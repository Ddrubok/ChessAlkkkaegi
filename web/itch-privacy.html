Chess Alkkagi Privacy

Solo stage run upgrades and checkpoints are saved in this browser's local storage. They can be removed by clearing site data. You can play as a guest, sign in with itch.io, or sign in with an existing email and password account. Signed-in account progress is synchronized through Supabase across devices. Guest stage progress is not automatically transferred to an account. An existing account can explicitly link an itch.io identity; linking does not merge accounts or transfer progress. Google sign-in is unavailable in this itch.io embed.

Itch.io sign-in opens a separate popup. The game sends a temporary authorization challenge to the Supabase sign-in service, which exchanges the itch.io authorization code, reads your itch.io profile identity, and returns an account session to this browser. The temporary sign-in attempt expires after five minutes. The game does not store your itch.io access token.

Friendly online play uses Supabase Realtime to exchange room codes and connection messages, then WebRTC to exchange game moves between players. Your browser contacts STUN servers and may use the production Metered TURN relay to establish a peer connection. A room code can be shared with another player; the itch.io game page may not pass its invite query into the embedded game, so the invited player may need to enter the code manually.

This edition does not load ads, analytics, CrazyGames, or Poki SDKs. Network traffic is used for account sign-in and sync, or when you choose online play.
