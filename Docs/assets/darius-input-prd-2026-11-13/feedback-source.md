> Hi! I did a bit more testing and wanted to share a few observations.
>
> In fullscreen mode, even at maximum zoom, the board takes up less than 30% of the screen. I wish I could zoom in further.

> screenshot
>
> As for the shooting mechanics, the power percentage indicator on the left makes it easier to understand how different power levels actually feel.
>
> And I think I've finally figured out what's been making aiming so difficult for me. Just to clarify, I'm testing the web version on a laptop, using a mouse.
>
> The entire range of shot power, from "the piece barely moves" to "the piece flies into outer space," is covered by just 80 pixels of mouse movement.
>
> And when aiming at around 30% power, changing the direction of the shot by 45 degrees requires moving the cursor by only about 12 pixels.
>
> This makes aiming feel unnecessarily demanding in terms of precision, at least for me. Every single pixel has a noticeable impact on either the shot power or its direction, so even small mouse movements can significantly change the result.
>
> I think I've finally managed to put into words, at least for myself, why the controls have felt so difficult to me.
