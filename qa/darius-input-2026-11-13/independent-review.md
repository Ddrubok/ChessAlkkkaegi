# Independent Darius source review (2026-11-13)

## Confirmed source finding

**Medium — the off-axis projection survives camera policy transitions.** `centerBoardProjection()` is called only by `fitCameraToBoard()` (`web/src/scene.ts:258-328`), which also runs on OrbitControls `change` and resize. `switchInputMode()` and selected-piece/strike transitions call `beginCameraRestore()` (`web/src/input.ts:665, 1489-1505`), and `updateInputRuntime()` animates camera position/target and changes the final polar/distance constraints directly (`web/src/input.ts:2603-2651`). That animation does not call `fitCameraToBoard()`, clear the view offset, or recenter it at completion. Thus a classic fixed-pitch view's nonzero offset is retained in the free billiards view, including the selected close-up, until a later controls change or resize. Conversely a billiards-to-classic transition can finish with the centered projection and stay that way until a controls change. This is a visual/camera policy regression, and the stale projection also affects pointer raycasting consistently with the *wrong* image position. Minimal fix: synchronize projection at policy transition start/end (clear it on entry to free/selected/strike; recenter it when the fixed board view settles), with no fit-induced camera movement during a power gesture. Cover both mode directions and selected/strike transitions in a live camera test.

## Other review observations

- No independent pointer-coordinate mismatch found in the fixed classic view: `raycastNearestPiece` and selected-surface raycasts use canvas-relative NDC with `Raycaster.setFromCamera`, which uses the camera projection; `Vector3.project` does too. The off-axis offset itself is coherent while the camera policy remains fixed.
- Mouse and pen gestures freeze exponent 2 and effective distance at pointer down; touch freezes exponent 1. Both classic and red-dot release paths sample the pointer-up coordinate. Reachability and the tutorial use the inverse curve. Settings calculate the actual 100% reference distance from the current canvas and show the selected preferred value, actual rounded value, and a limit note. No physical-rule changes appear in the reviewed diff.
- `playtest-frame-check.mjs` extracts the actual camera and power helper source and checks board corners, not piece hulls, pointer picking, mode transitions, or release validity. Its algebraic power check uses inverse and forward functions from the same implementation, so it is a consistency check, not independent proof of the desired UX. `itch-power-controls-check.mjs` exercises final release, touch linearity and settings cancellation with mocks; its red-dot quadratic path constructs a gesture directly, so it does not prove production pointer-down routing.
- The available `after-01/result.json` browser artifact reports board-height and all-piece-hull checks passing at its tested viewports, and no page errors. It also reports failed `selected` assertions because the script's requested pawn resolves to a king in several cases; those failures do not by themselves establish a gameplay bug. The 390x844 resize-cancellation assertion fails while its subsequent release queues zero shots. This review did not rerun or debug that browser script, so the resize assertion remains an artifact-level open question, not a confirmed source defect.
