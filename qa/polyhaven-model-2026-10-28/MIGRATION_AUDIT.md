## P2 — reuse or adapt focused geometry and physics checks

1. **Conversion contract and base stability:** `web/src/tools/convert.ts` generates six meshes, King normalized to height 1, collider points, flattened base (`BASE_FLATTEN_EPS = 0.004`), at least 12 base hull points and a 48-bin rim; it currently hardcodes the old FBX SHA-256 and `source.file: "chess.fbx"`. The converter worker must change source assumptions for Poly Haven before treating a success as evidence. Inspect per-piece `bounds.y`, `baseRadius`, `basePointCount`, `baseHullPointCount`, and collider min Y/visual min Y. A numerically valid hull may still stand on a thin horse neck or wobble after landing. `web/src/assets.ts` allows 4–400 collider points; watch this if the converter changes its budget.
2. **Real Rapier outcomes:** `web/src/tools/collider-physics-harness.mjs` offers `--mode check|q1|q2|q3|q4|all` and `--output`. Q1 probes tipping over 36 azimuths, Q2 travel/settle, Q3 hull fit, Q4 all 36 piece-pair collisions. Example after adapting the harness: `cd web; node src/tools/collider-physics-harness.mjs --mode check --output ../qa/polyhaven-model-2026-10-28/collider-check.json`; then targeted Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4. **As written this is an old-versus-HEAD collider comparison requiring exactly 400 points in both inputs, not a drop-in Poly Haven pass gate.** `collider-physics-harness-448.mjs` instead expects 400 old/448 new and is also tied to an earlier experiment. Both write a JSON and sibling Markdown report; use another output directory if preserving this audit's single-file ownership boundary.
3. **Gameplay balance and landings:** `node src/tools/classic-balance-check.mjs`, `node src/tools/balance-physics-check.mjs`, `node src/tools/stage-headless-check.mjs`, and `node src/tools/piece-preview-contract-check.mjs` consume current metadata/GLB and cover real launch mass/speed, stage behavior, and preview framing. `balance.csv` values can become wrong when hull volume/height changes; compare masses, travel, tipping, edge falls and pawn/knight landing visually in gameplay, not only assertions. `web/src/physics.ts` uses collider points and `bounds.y` for spawn/settle and map pockets; geometry height changes can alter obstacle and pocket clearance.
4. **Knight facing:** `web/src/layout.ts` places white Knights at b1/g1 and black at b8/g8. `web/src/scene.ts` copies Rapier body quaternion directly to the visual mesh; there is no automatic aesthetic correction after conversion. Inspect front direction at both starts, from the player camera and during a jump/landing. A correctly named `Knight` mesh may face sideways/backward if the new source axis differs. Check its collider/visual orientation together so contact and silhouette match.

No tests were run by this read-only worker. The audit did not inspect an authenticated portal upload or a deployed CDN. Historical QA folders (`qa/**`), dated candidate captures and the cinematic film projects under `store-assets/cinetic-*/` contain independent old source copies/previews; they are **not copied into the current web/Android/CrazyGames game pipelines**. Keep them as dated evidence, but do not attach their old rendered media or editable ZIPs to a new marketing release without reauthoring from the replacement model.
